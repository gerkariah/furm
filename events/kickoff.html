The Kickoff Social is casual, warm, and low-pressure. It's less an info session and more a chance to actually meet the FURM community. Come as you are, stay as long as you like, and leave knowing a few new faces.

Everyone. First-years looking for community, upperclassmen who want to reconnect, and anyone curious about FURM. Whether you're first-generation, underrepresented in medicine or research, or an ally who believes in more welcoming healthcare spaces, there's a seat for you here.
