What it is

A panel-style discussion with medical students, including former FURM and PLME students, about what med school is actually like. We keep it conversational, open, and real, so you get a genuine picture instead of an intimidating or overly polished one. The whole point: ask the questions you actually want answered.

What we'll talk about

Honest ground, especially the stuff people don't usually say out loud:

Finances & the real cost Finding community Work-life balance Imposter syndrome Identity in medicine Mental health Academic pressure What they wish they'd known

Ask anything

Some of the questions this event is built to answer:

"How am I going to afford this?"
"Will I find community?"
"Am I smart enough for med school?"
"How bad is the workload, really?"
"Do you still have free time or hobbies?"
"What do you wish people were more honest about?"
