What CubCare is

CubCare pairs FURM underclassmen with upperclassmen for real connection, support, and guidance. It's less about formal mentorship and more about community: friendship, belonging, and helping each other navigate premed and PLME without feeling alone. Little bears and big bears, looking out for one another.

How pairing works

Good pairings matter far more than random matches, so we're intentional about it. We pair based on:

  • Specialty and academic interests
  • Hobbies and personality
  • Identity and community background
  • Extracurricular involvement

The goal is a pairing that feels comfortable, intentional, and built to last, not just a name over email.

The pairing event

In October, we bring everyone together to actually meet, not just swap emails. Warm, low-pressure, community-first.

  1. Arrive & mingle over snacks
  2. Short intro to CubCare
  3. Pairing announcements
  4. Guided conversation with your match
  5. Casual hangout afterward
