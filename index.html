Who we are

FURM is a home within Brown PLME for students who care about community, mentorship, and equity in healthcare. We're first-generation and underrepresented in research and medicine — and YOU get to define what that means for YOU. Whoever you are, if you believe in building more welcoming healthcare spaces, there's a place for you here.

More than anything, we're about belonging and community: real connection, trust, and support as you find your people and your footing in medicine. Come learn about us, come to an event, come as you are.
